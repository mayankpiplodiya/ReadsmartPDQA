The internet has created an explosion of information in the form of blogs, news articles, research papers, policy documents, and reports. While this is beneficial, it often overwhelms readers who must go through large amounts of content to extract useful insights.
Students spend hours reading long academic papers, professionals review detailed reports, and government officers deal with hundreds of pages of policy drafts.

ReadSmart, was conceptualized to address this challenge. It provides a tool that reduces the time and effort required to read lengthy content by generating short, accurate, and coherent summaries.
